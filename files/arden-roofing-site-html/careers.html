Careers

Arden Roofing looks for dependable people who take pride in physical work, communicate well with a crew, and understand that good roofing depends on details that customers may never see.

What We Look For

  • Care about the work. We want people who take pride in doing roofing replacement & repair properly, not just quickly.
  • Straight talk with customers. You will be in someone's home or business explaining what you found. Plain language beats jargon.
  • Reliability. Showing up when you said you would matters more here than any single line on a resume.
  • Respect for the property. Protecting the space, cleaning up, and leaving it better than you found it.
  • Safety, every time. Following safe practice is part of the job, not an extra.

Experience in roofing replacement & repair or a related trade helps. If you have the attitude and not yet the trade experience, say so, because we do train.

Roles We Hire For

We are a small team, so openings come and go with the work. These are the roles we hire into.

Roofing Installer

Work with the field crew on residential roof installation and replacement projects, including tear-off, material handling, underlayment, shingle installation, flashing support, and jobsite cleanup.

Qualifications

  • Comfortable with outdoor physical work and changing weather conditions
  • Able to follow jobsite safety procedures and crew direction
  • Dependable transportation and consistent attendance
  • Roofing or construction experience is helpful but not required for every opening

Benefits

  • On-the-job skill development
  • Consistent local and regional project work
  • Opportunities to take on additional crew responsibility
  • Team environment focused on organized, careful work

Roof Repair Technician

Inspect and complete residential repair work involving shingles, flashing, roof penetrations, valleys, transitions, and other localized roofing problems.

Qualifications

  • Previous roofing or exterior construction experience
  • Comfort working on residential roofs of varying pitches
  • Ability to think through likely water-entry paths
  • Clear communication with project leads and homeowners

Benefits

  • Varied repair work rather than repetitive production only
  • Opportunity to build advanced diagnostic skills
  • Local service-area projects
  • Room to grow into field leadership

Roofing Project Lead

Help organize residential roofing projects from material staging through completion, coordinate the crew, watch key installation details, and communicate field conditions that affect the planned scope.

Qualifications

  • Strong residential roofing experience
  • Ability to lead a small crew and keep work organized
  • Working knowledge of shingles, underlayment, flashing, and roof transitions
  • Good judgment around jobsite safety, weather, access, and property protection

Benefits

  • Leadership responsibility on local projects
  • Opportunity to help train developing installers
  • Direct communication with company ownership
  • Varied work across the Asheville region

Customer & Project Coordinator

Support customer communication, estimate follow-up, scheduling, project notes, and coordination between homeowners and the field team.

Qualifications

  • Strong written and verbal communication
  • Comfort managing schedules and detailed project notes
  • Reliable follow-through
  • Experience in home services, construction, or customer support is helpful

Benefits

  • Work closely with a small local team
  • Learn the residential roofing process
  • Meaningful role in keeping customers informed
  • Opportunities to take on broader operations responsibilities

How to Apply

Email sales@ardenroofing.com with "Careers" and the role in the subject line. Include:

  • A short note about the kind of work you have done
  • Your resume, or simply a list of previous jobs and dates
  • The area you can reliably get to
  • A phone number and the best time to reach you

We read every application. If there is a fit, expect a short phone conversation first, then a meeting and a ride-along on a job so you can see the work before you commit to it.

We do not always have an opening posted. Send your information anyway. We keep applications on file and reach out first to people who already introduced themselves when a spot opens.
